Band Descriptors — what examiners actually mark

Writing — 4 criteria, each 25%

Task Response (Task 2) / Task Achievement (Task 1)

BandPlain English
9Fully addresses every part. Position clear & well-developed. Ideas extended with relevance.
8Sufficiently addresses every part. Well-developed response. Clear position throughout.
7Addresses all parts. Clear position throughout. Main ideas extended & supported (but may over-generalise or under-extend at points).
6Addresses all parts but some more than others. Position present but unclear or repetitive. Ideas relevant but not always extended.
5Addresses task only partially. Format may be inappropriate. Position unclear; main ideas limited & not sufficiently developed.

Coherence & Cohesion

9Skilful management of paragraphing. Cohesion attracts no attention.
8Sequenced information logically. Cohesive devices used well, with occasional over/under-use. Clear central topic per paragraph.
7Logically organised, clear progression. Range of cohesive devices used appropriately, though there may be some under/over-use. Each paragraph has a clear topic.
6Information coherently arranged. Cohesive devices used, but not always appropriately. Paragraphing may be ineffective.
5Some organisation but lacks clear progression. Cohesion inadequate or mechanical. Inadequate paragraphing.
Critical: Band 7 CC says cohesion "may be over/under-used." Mechanical 5+ connectors → Band 6 ceiling.

Lexical Resource

9Wide range used naturally, accurately. Rare minor errors.
8Wide range, fluently & flexibly. Skilful use of uncommon items, occasional inaccuracy in word choice / collocation.
7Sufficient range for flexibility & precision. Some less common items + awareness of style & collocation. Some errors in word choice / spelling, but they do not impede.
6Adequate range for the task. Some flexibility. Errors in spelling / word formation but do not impede.
5Limited range. Frequent errors that may cause some difficulty for reader.

Grammatical Range & Accuracy

9Wide range, used accurately & flexibly. Rare minor errors.
8Wide range. Majority error-free. Occasional inappropriacies.
7Variety of complex structures. Frequent error-free sentences. Good control of grammar & punctuation, but a few errors.
6Mix of simple & complex. Some errors, but meaning rarely blocked.
5Limited range of structures. Frequent errors that cause difficulty.

Speaking — 4 criteria, each 25%

Fluency & Coherence

9Fluent. Coherent. Topic developed fully & appropriately.
8Fluent with only occasional repetition / self-correction. Hesitation is content-related, not language-related.
7Speaks at length without noticeable effort. Some language-related hesitation. Uses cohesive devices flexibly.
6Willing to speak at length, but loses coherence sometimes. Repetition / self-correction. Cohesion sometimes overused.
5Maintains flow despite frequent hesitations. Over-uses connectives.

Lexical Resource

9Full flexibility & precision in all topics. Idiomatic language used naturally & accurately.
8Wide vocabulary fluently & flexibly. Skilful use of less common & idiomatic items.
7Flexibility on a range of topics. Some less common items. Effective paraphrase.
6Wide enough vocabulary to discuss most topics. Generally paraphrases successfully.
5Manages most familiar topics. Limited flexibility, attempt at paraphrase mixed.

Grammatical Range & Accuracy

9Full range used naturally & appropriately. Rare minor errors.
8Wide range, flexible. Most sentences error-free.
7Range of complex structures with flexibility. Frequent error-free sentences. Some persistent errors.
6Mix of simple & complex. Errors with complex structures rarely block meaning.
5Basic forms with reasonable accuracy. Limited complex structures, often with errors.

Pronunciation

9Full range of features used precisely & subtly. Effortlessly intelligible.
8Wide range of features used effectively. L1 accent has minimal effect.
7Range of pron features used with mixed control. Sustained ability shown but not throughout. Generally intelligible; L1 accent has limited effect.
6Range of features but mixed control. Generally clear pronunciation, though mispronunciations cause occasional comprehension issues.
5Limited range used. Mispronunciations cause some difficulty for listener.
