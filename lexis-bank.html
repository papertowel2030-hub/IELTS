Lexis Bank — what to learn, in what order

Band 7+ Lexical Resource is not "advanced words." It is: precision + collocation + paraphrase + topic-specific lexis. Learn 5–10 collocations per day, in context, in Anki.

Layer 1: Academic Word List (AWL) — 570 word families

Coxhead's AWL covers ~10% of academic English. Master sublists 1–4 (the highest-frequency 240 words) for IELTS. Free word lists + Anki decks:

  • UEFAP — AWL by sublist
  • Anki: search "AWL" in the shared decks. Use one with example sentences, not isolated words.

Layer 2: Collocations (the Band 7+ differentiator)

Collocations are word partnerships native speakers use ("make a decision", not "do a decision"). Examiners reward natural collocation in LR.

Verb + nounAdjective + nounVerb + adverb
make a decision · take a risk · meet a deadline · raise concerns · pose a threat · play a role · cause damage · provide evidenceheavy traffic · valid point · significant impact · widespread belief · sustainable growth · vested interest · pressing issue · adverse effectstrongly agree · widely accepted · firmly believe · severely affected · radically change · sharply increase · gradually decline

Authoritative free resource: ozdic.com (Oxford Collocations).

Layer 3: Topic clusters (10 sets)

Build 15–25 items per cluster. Anki one cluster per week.

1. Environment / climate

greenhouse gases · carbon footprint · emissions · renewable energy · fossil fuels · biodiversity · deforestation · ecosystem · sustainability · conservation · pollution · global warming · climate change · ecological balance · single-use plastics · circular economy

Collocations: tackle climate change · reduce emissions · preserve biodiversity · adopt sustainable practices · impose carbon tax

2. Technology / internet / social media

digital divide · screen time · social media platforms · algorithm · misinformation · cybersecurity · automation · artificial intelligence · privacy concerns · digital literacy · echo chamber · online presence · streaming

Collocations: raise privacy concerns · combat misinformation · embrace automation · widen the digital divide · regulate platforms

3. Education

compulsory education · vocational training · tuition fees · academic performance · critical thinking · rote learning · higher education · pedagogy · curriculum · student-centred · lifelong learning · drop-out rate · scholarship

Collocations: foster critical thinking · pursue higher education · cover the curriculum · earn a scholarship · drop out of school

4. Health / lifestyle

sedentary lifestyle · obesity · chronic disease · mental health · well-being · processed food · public health · life expectancy · preventive medicine · burnout · work-life balance · vaccination

Collocations: lead a sedentary lifestyle · raise awareness · combat obesity · maintain a balanced diet · suffer from burnout

5. Work / career

remote work · gig economy · job satisfaction · workforce · upskilling · career progression · workplace culture · burnout · automation · redundancy · entrepreneurship · soft skills

Collocations: climb the career ladder · meet a deadline · take on responsibility · be made redundant · cultivate soft skills

6. Hometown / urban planning / travel

urbanisation · congestion · infrastructure · public transport · green spaces · gentrification · suburban sprawl · cost of living · cultural heritage · tourism boom · ecotourism

Collocations: ease congestion · preserve cultural heritage · invest in infrastructure · boost local tourism · drive gentrification

7. Family / relationships

extended family · nuclear family · upbringing · generation gap · parenting styles · independence · childcare · ageing population · gender roles

Collocations: bridge the generation gap · share household responsibilities · provide emotional support · raise children · support an ageing population

8. Media / news / globalisation

9. Crime / law

10. Hobbies / leisure / arts

Layer 5: Linkers — used with restraint

FunctionExamples (use sparingly + appropriately)
Addingmoreover, furthermore, in addition, also, what's more
Contrastinghowever, nevertheless, conversely, on the contrary, whereas, while
Causaltherefore, consequently, as a result, thus, hence, owing to
Exemplifyingfor instance, to illustrate, a case in point, take X for example
Concessionadmittedly, granted, while it is true that, of course
Conclusionin summary, on balance, all things considered, to sum up

Rule: if you wrote 5 of these in 250 words, delete 2. Replace with reference (this, such, the latter) or punctuation.

Anki workflow (daily)

  1. Add 5 new cards per day, MAX. Quality > quantity.
  2. Card front: collocation in a sentence, target word blanked.
  3. Card back: filled sentence + 1-line meaning + 1 example of your own.
  4. Review 15 min/day. Skip review = cards balloon = give up. Discipline matters more than volume.
