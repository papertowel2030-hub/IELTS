Listening — Target Band 8.0

Get 36 of 40 questions correct. Audio plays only ONCE. Paper test = 10 min transfer; computer-based = 2 min review. Know which you booked.

Accent training (mandatory for Band 8+)

Core drills (rotate daily — 15 min each)

Drill 1: Shadowing (fluency + sound discrimination)

Play a 60-sec clip. Repeat aloud 1–2 sec behind speaker. Match rhythm, stress, pauses. Do not look at the transcript first. Repeat 3×.

Drill 2: Dictation (Section 1 form-fill accuracy)

Play a 30-sec address/phone/date clip. Pause. Write everything. Check against transcript. Track spelling errors in your error log.

Drill 3: Prediction (Section 4 outline notes)

Open a Section 4 question set. Before audio: predict the part of speech of each gap (noun? verb? number?). Then listen. Builds top-down processing.

Drill 4: Speed-spell (numbers, names, places)

Have a partner / TTS spell 20 names + 10 phone numbers + 10 postcodes. Write under time pressure. The cheapest band-saver in the test.

Hard rules (memorize)

  • Answers come mostly in order. Exception: matching tasks, MCQ with multiple correct.
  • If you miss one, abandon it instantly. Do NOT freeze.
  • Spelling counts. "Wendnesday" = 0 marks. British English standard (colour, organisation) — American spellings accepted.
  • Plurals matter. "Book" ≠ "books". Listen for the /s/ or /z/.
  • Numbers: write digits. "2:30" not "two thirty". Faster, fewer errors.
  • Word limit is absolute. "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER" — 3 words = 0.
  • Capitals only required on proper nouns. Safest = write everything in CAPS to avoid /h/ vs /H/ ambiguity.

Section 4 — the band-killer

One 5-min academic lecture, 10 questions, no break to re-read. Most candidates drop 4+ marks here.

  • Pre-read all 10 questions in the 30-sec gap. Group by subtopic.
  • Lecture structure cues: "Today I'll cover…", "Moving on to…", "Finally…". Use them as anchors.
  • The lecturer signposts the answer with stress + slow-down. Train your ear to catch the slow-down.
  • Section 4 vocabulary clusters: research methods, history, biology, sociology, urban planning. Pre-learn 5 lexis sets — see lexis bank.

Daily routine (15 min weekday / 60 min weekend)

Step 1: Pick one section from Cambridge IELTS 17–19 (priority) or British Council.
Step 2: 30-sec pre-read of the questions. Underline keywords, predict word class.
Step 3: Play ONCE at normal speed. Do not pause. Write on the question booklet.
Step 4: Mark answers. For every wrong one, log to error log with a category (mishearing / spelling / wrong word class / distractor / out-of-order).
Step 5: Replay with transcript. Find the exact word you missed and the cue that preceded it. Re-listen to that 10-sec chunk 3×.

Common mistakes that drop your band

  • Stopping when you miss one (cascade: lose the next 2–3 too).
  • Reading the question while audio plays for a previous question.
  • Capitalisation panic — wastes seconds. Default to all-CAPS.
  • Overwriting the word limit ("a desk lamp" when limit is 2 words = 0).
  • Only training one accent.
