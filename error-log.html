Error Log — the single biggest lever in this guide

Every wrong answer gets a row. Each row has a reason category. Categories drive next week's drills. Without this, you re-make the same mistake for 8 weeks.

Why this works (learning science in one paragraph)

Retrieval practice + error analysis + spaced re-attempt is the most studied "what works in studying" trio (Roediger, Karpicke, Bjork). The act of writing why you got it wrong, naming the category, and re-attempting after 48 hours moves the correction into long-term memory far better than re-reading.

Reason categories by skill

Listening

  • Mishearing — wrong word due to accent or speed. Drill: accent variety + shadowing.
  • Spelling — heard correctly, wrote it wrong. Drill: dictation 5 min/day, common-misspell list.
  • Plural / form — heard "books" wrote "book" or vice versa. Drill: plural-marker listening drills.
  • Word class — gap needed noun, wrote verb. Drill: pre-listen prediction of part of speech.
  • Distractor — picked the first-mentioned option in MCQ. Drill: "wait for confirmation" practice.
  • Out-of-order — assumed linear order in a matching task. Drill: rubric-reading discipline.
  • Word-limit violation — wrote 3 words when max was 2. Drill: highlight word limit every time.
  • Lost place — froze on Q5, missed Q6+Q7. Drill: "abandon and move" drills.

Reading

  • Paraphrase missed — didn't see the synonym. Drill: daily paraphrase pairs from lexis bank.
  • NG vs F — confused silence for contradiction (or vice versa). Drill: re-read NG decision tree on reading page.
  • Quantifier slip — "all" in question vs "most" in passage. Drill: highlight quantifiers (all, most, some, often, rarely, never).
  • Outside knowledge — answered from what you know, not the text. Drill: finger-on-evidence rule.
  • Time pressure — guessed in last 5 min. Drill: pacing per section.
  • Wrong question type tactic — used "questions first" on Matching Headings. Drill: tactic table per type.
  • Grammar fit — answer didn't fit grammatically. Drill: word-class prediction before reading.

Writing

  • TR — bullet missed (T1) or question part missed (T2).
  • TR — no clear position.
  • TR — ideas stated but not extended.
  • CC — mechanical connectors.
  • CC — paragraph without clear topic sentence.
  • LR — repetition of basic word.
  • LR — wrong collocation.
  • LR — inappropriate "advanced" word.
  • GRA — same simple structure repeated.
  • GRA — error in complex sentence.
  • GRA — article / subject-verb agreement / tense.
  • Length — under/over word count.

Speaking

  • FC — long hesitation for content (not language).
  • FC — fillers ("um", "you know", "like").
  • LR — basic word repeated.
  • LR — wrong collocation.
  • GRA — sentence broken mid-clause.
  • GRA — narrow range, all simple sentences.
  • Pron — monotone, no sentence stress.
  • Pron — sound-level errors that blocked the examiner.
  • Strategy — didn't cover all bullets in P2.
  • Strategy — one-word P1 answer.

Weekly review (Friday or Sunday, 30 min)

  1. Read every row from the week.
  2. Count by category. Which 2 categories appear most often?
  3. Those 2 are next week's targeted drills. Block 60% of practice time on them.
  4. Re-attempt every "—" in the 48hr column. Mark ✓ / ✗.
  5. If a category appears 3 weeks running → method is wrong, not effort. Get external feedback.

Anki integration (vocabulary errors)

Every LR error (unknown word + correct collocation + lost paraphrase) becomes an Anki card. Card format:

  • Front: sentence with the target word blanked.
  • Back: the word + 1-line definition + the original sentence.

Review 5 min/day. Don't make new cards faster than you can review.
