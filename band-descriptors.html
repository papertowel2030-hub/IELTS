Band Descriptors — what examiners actually mark

Most candidates study for months without ever reading the rubric they're marked against. Skim this once now, then come back whenever you self-score. You don't need to memorise it.

✍️ Writing — 4 criteria, each worth 25%

Your Writing band is the average of these four. A weakness in any one drags the whole score down — so train the lowest one.

Task Response / Task Achievement ›
9Fully addresses every part. Position clear & well-developed. Ideas extended with relevance.
8Sufficiently addresses every part. Well-developed response. Clear position throughout.
7Addresses all parts. Clear position throughout. Main ideas extended & supported (may over-generalise or under-extend at points).
6Addresses all parts but some more than others. Position present but unclear/repetitive. Ideas relevant but not always extended.
5Addresses task only partially. Format may be inappropriate. Position unclear; ideas limited & underdeveloped.
Coherence & Cohesion ›
9Skilful paragraphing. Cohesion attracts no attention.
8Logically sequenced. Cohesive devices used well, occasional over/under-use. Clear central topic per paragraph.
7Logically organised, clear progression. Range of cohesive devices used appropriately, some under/over-use. Each paragraph has a clear topic.
6Coherently arranged. Cohesive devices used but not always appropriately. Paragraphing may be ineffective.
5Some organisation but lacks progression. Cohesion inadequate or mechanical. Weak paragraphing.
The Band-7 trap: the rubric says cohesion "may be over/under-used." Spraying 5+ linkers ("Moreover, Furthermore, In addition…") reads as mechanical → Band 6 ceiling. Connect ideas with reference and structure, not just connectors.
Lexical Resource (vocabulary) ›
9Wide range used naturally & accurately. Rare minor errors.
8Wide range, fluent & flexible. Skilful use of uncommon items; occasional slip in word choice/collocation.
7Enough range for flexibility & precision. Some less common items + awareness of collocation. Occasional errors that don't impede.
6Adequate range. Some flexibility. Errors in spelling/word formation but don't impede.
5Limited range. Frequent errors that may cause difficulty for the reader.
Myth: "advanced words" raise your band. Truth: Band 7 rewards collocation and precision ("ease congestion", "a valid point") — not thesaurus words dropped in wrongly.
Grammatical Range & Accuracy ›
9Wide range, accurate & flexible. Rare minor errors.
8Wide range. Majority error-free. Occasional inappropriacies.
7Variety of complex structures. Frequent error-free sentences. Good control, but a few errors.
6Mix of simple & complex. Some errors, but meaning rarely blocked.
5Limited range. Frequent errors that cause difficulty.

🗣️ Speaking — 4 criteria, each worth 25%

You'll drill these in live sessions you book with me. Know what "Band 7" sounds like:

Fluency & Coherence ›
9Fluent, coherent, fully developed.
8Fluent; only occasional repetition/self-correction. Hesitation is about ideas, not language.
7Speaks at length without noticeable effort. Some language-related hesitation. Flexible cohesive devices.
6Willing to speak at length but loses coherence at times. Repetition; some overused connectives.
5Keeps going despite frequent hesitation. Over-uses connectives.
Lexical Resource ›
9Full flexibility & precision. Natural, accurate idiom.
8Wide vocabulary, fluent & flexible. Skilful less-common & idiomatic use.
7Flexibility across a range of topics. Some less common items. Effective paraphrase.
6Enough vocabulary to discuss most topics. Generally paraphrases successfully.
5Manages familiar topics; limited flexibility.
Grammatical Range & Accuracy ›
9Full range, natural & appropriate. Rare minor errors.
8Wide range, flexible. Most sentences error-free.
7Range of complex structures with flexibility. Frequent error-free sentences. Some persistent errors.
6Mix of simple & complex. Errors rarely block meaning.
5Basic forms with reasonable accuracy; limited complex structures.
Pronunciation ›
9Full range of features, precise & subtle. Effortless to understand.
8Wide range used effectively. Accent has minimal effect.
7Range of features with mixed control. Generally easy to understand; accent has limited effect.
6Some features, mixed control. Generally clear, occasional lapses.
5Limited features. Mispronunciations sometimes make you hard to follow.
Pronunciation ≠ accent. You keep your accent. Examiners mark stress, rhythm, and intonation — the music of English, not sounding "native".
