Listening — Target Band 8.0

Get 36 of 40 questions correct. Audio plays only ONCE. No second chances.

Test format (30 min + 10 min transfer)

  • Section 1 — social conversation (easy)
  • Section 2 — monologue, everyday topic (medium)
  • Section 3 — academic discussion, 3–4 speakers (hard)
  • Section 4 — academic lecture (hardest)
  • 40 questions total

How to study — daily steps

Step 1: Open British Council Listening tests. Pick one section. Play it ONCE.
Step 2: Read the questions BEFORE the audio starts. Underline keywords.
Step 3: Write answers while listening. Do not stop the audio.
Step 4: Check answers. Mark every wrong answer in a notebook.
Step 5: Replay the audio. Find where you missed the answer in the transcript.

Top 5 rules

  • Answers come IN ORDER. If you missed one, move on. Don't stop.
  • Spelling counts. "Wendnesday" = wrong. Practice common words.
  • Plurals matter. "Book" ≠ "books".
  • Numbers: write digits, not words (faster).
  • Capitals on names and places only.

Question types — practice each one

  • Multiple choice — beware distractors (speaker says X, then "actually no, Y")
  • Form / note completion — read word limit ("NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS")
  • Map labelling — orient yourself first, follow speaker's directions
  • Matching — eliminate wrong options as you hear them
  • Sentence completion — grammar must fit

Common mistakes that drop your band

  • Stopping when you miss one answer (you lose the next 3 too)
  • Not reading questions before audio
  • Wrong spelling on easy words
  • Writing too much (only 1–3 words usually allowed)
