Step 6: Test day checklist

Click to expand — 10 essential items
  • Bring passport (same one used at registration)
  • Arrive 30 minutes early
  • Bring water in a clear, label-free bottle
  • Phone OFF (not silent — fully off)
  • Pen + pencil + eraser (HB pencil for answer sheets)
  • Use the bathroom before the test starts
  • Do NOT talk to anyone during the test
  • Use the first minute to scan all questions
  • Write Listening answers directly on the answer sheet
  • Stay calm in Speaking — examiner is neutral, not judging you personally
