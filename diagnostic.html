🩺 Diagnostic

Don't study blind

One full mock → score every section → see your gap to target → the app points your effort where it counts. Re-do this every ~3–4 weeks to track real movement.

Step 1 · Take one full mock

Use Cambridge IELTS 17, Test 1 (save the newest book for later). Do Listening + Reading + Writing back-to-back under real timing; record your Speaking the same day with a 1-minute Part 2 prep.
Hand-write Writing on lined paper if your real test is on paper.

Step 2 · Score it

Listening & Reading: count correct answers, convert with the table on Band Descriptors.
Writing & Speaking: self-score against the descriptors — but treat it as rough. Self-scoring is unreliable above Band 6, so get one external check when you can.

Step 3 · Record your scores

This updates your dashboard so "Do this next" targets your real weak spots.

Step 4 · How to weight your week

Gap to targetRoughly how much of your week
At or above targetLight maintenance only
0.5 band belowA small daily slice
1.0 band belowA big share — this is a priority skill
1.5+ band belowThe largest share; consider giving yourself more weeks

Step 5 · If a skill is stuck after a re-test

Listening → add accent variety + shadowing.
Reading → drill paraphrase recognition + AWL vocabulary.
Writing → get examiner/AI feedback; you've likely been self-marking against the wrong bar.
Speaking → record and analyse against descriptors, and book a live session.
Reschedule rule: if a late mock still misses your target by more than 0.5 in any skill, move the test. A few extra weeks of focused prep is far cheaper than a retake.
