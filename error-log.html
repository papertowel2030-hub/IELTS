📖 What the reason categories mean (drill guide) ›

Each reason links to a specific fix. When a reason keeps recurring, that's exactly what to practise.

Listening: misheard → accent + shadowing · spelling → dictation drills · plural/form → listen for the /s/ · wrong word class → predict part of speech · distractor → wait for confirmation · out-of-order → read the rubric · word-limit → highlight the limit · lost place → "abandon & move on".

Reading: paraphrase missed → paraphrase drill · NG-vs-False → the decision tree · quantifier slip → highlight all/most/some · outside knowledge → finger on the evidence · time pressure → pacing · wrong tactic → the type table.

Writing: task not fully answered · no clear position · idea not extended · mechanical connectors · weak topic sentence · basic word repeated · wrong collocation · inappropriate "advanced" word · same structure repeated · error in complex sentence · wrong article/tense/agreement · under/over length.

Speaking: hesitation for language · fillers · basic word repeated · wrong collocation · broken sentence · all-simple sentences · monotone / no stress · sound blocked the listener · missed a cue-card bullet · one-word Part 1 answer.

Weekly review (30 min, once a week): read the week's rows, look at your top-2 pattern above, and put 60% of next week's practice there. Re-attempt anything you haven't marked "fixed". If a reason recurs for 3 weeks, the method is off — bring it to a session or get feedback.
