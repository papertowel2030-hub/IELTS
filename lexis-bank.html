📚 Lexis Bank

What to learn — in what order

Band 7+ vocabulary isn't "big words". It's precision + collocation + paraphrase + topic lexis. Learn 5–10 collocations a day, in context. Send the useful ones to your Flashcards.

Tip: don't try to swallow this page. Pick one topic cluster per week (the one your next essay/speaking topic needs) and drill just that.

Layer 1 · Collocations — the real Band-7 differentiator ›

Word partnerships native speakers use ("make a decision", not "do a decision"). This is what examiners reward as "natural" vocabulary.

Layer 2 · Academic Word List (AWL) ›

Coxhead's AWL (570 word families) covers ~10% of academic English. Prioritise sublists 1–4 (the highest-frequency 240). Best for Reading and Academic Writing.
