Diagnostic — baseline + study weights

Don't study blind. One full mock → score every section → calculate gap-to-target → weight your weekly hours accordingly.

Step 1: Take a full mock

  • Use Cambridge IELTS 17, Test 1 (don't burn the latest book in Week 1).
  • Real conditions: 2 hr 45 min straight. Listening + Reading + Writing back-to-back. Speaking later same day, recorded.
  • Hand-write Writing on lined paper. Use the official answer sheet PDF.
  • For Speaking: read all 3 parts aloud as a recording, with 1-min Part 2 prep.

Step 2: Score

  • Listening & Reading: count correct answers. Use the band-conversion table in descriptors page.
  • Writing: self-score against the band descriptors (TR/CC/LR/GRA). Be honest. If unsure → submit for external feedback (see feedback paths). Self-score is unreliable past Band 6.
  • Speaking: replay recording, score against the 4 criteria. Same caveat — submit for an external grade.

Step 4: Hours allocation (decision tree)

You have ~10 hrs / week (5 weekday × 30 min + 7.5 hrs weekend). Distribute by gap size.

GapWeekly hours on that skill
Already at target0.5 hr (maintenance only)

1.0 band below3 hrs
1.5+ band below4+ hrs and consider delaying the test

Step 5: Pick a track

Diagnostic profileRecommendation
All 4 skills within 0.5 band of target8-week plan as-is. Book test for Week 9.
1–2 skills 1.0 band below8-week plan, weight 60% on weak skills.
3+ skills 1.0 band below OR any skill 1.5+ below12-week plan. Re-diagnose at Week 4 + Week 8.
Writing OR Speaking 1.5+ below targetProductive-skill problems need external feedback. Self-study alone won't move them.

Step 6: Re-diagnose at Week 4

Full mock again. New gap analysis. Adjust weights. If a skill hasn't moved → the bottleneck is method, not hours. Switch tactics:

  • Listening stuck → add accent variety + shadowing.
  • Reading stuck → drill paraphrase recognition + AWL.
  • Writing stuck → submit for examiner feedback; you've been self-marking against the wrong rubric.
  • Speaking stuck → record and analyse against descriptors; or get conversation partner.

Reschedule rule

If Week 7 mock misses target by >0.5 in any skill → reschedule. One month extra weekend prep = realistic 0.5 band gain. Cheaper than a retake fee + lost time.
