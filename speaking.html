Speaking — Target Band 7.5

11–14 min face-to-face interview. 3 parts. Recorded. Examiner is neutral. You need: fluent delivery + wide vocabulary + complex grammar + clear pronunciation.

Test format

  • Part 1 (4–5 min): Intro + everyday questions (work, hobbies, hometown)
  • Part 2 (3–4 min): Cue card. 1 min prep. Speak 1–2 min. Examiner does NOT interrupt.
  • Part 3 (4–5 min): Discussion of abstract ideas linked to Part 2 topic

Part 1 strategy

Do not give 1-word answers. Give 2–3 sentences.
Add a reason: "Yes, I like cooking BECAUSE it relaxes me after work."
Add an example: "For instance, last Sunday I made…"
Stay natural. Don't memorize answers — examiner can tell.

Part 2 strategy (cue card)

Use the 1-minute prep to write 4–5 keywords on the paper.
Cover ALL bullet points on the card.
Speak for the FULL 1–2 minutes. If you stop early, your band drops.
Structure: Introduce → 2–3 details → Personal reflection → Final thought.

Part 3 strategy

Give opinion + reason + example: "I think X because Y. For example, Z."
Do not give 1-sentence answers. Develop your idea.
Use phrases: "In my view…", "It depends on…", "On the other hand…"
It's OK to disagree with the examiner — be respectful but show your view.

Band 7.5 self-assessment (record yourself + check)

CriterionBand 7.5 needs
FluencySpeaks at length with few hesitations. Self-corrects without panic.
VocabularyUses less common words ("intriguing" not "interesting"). 2–3 idiomatic phrases used naturally.
GrammarMix of simple + complex sentences. Few errors that don't block meaning.
PronunciationClear individual sounds. Uses stress + intonation. Easy to understand throughout.

How to study — daily steps

Step 1: Get a cue card from IELTS Liz — 100 Part 2 topics.
Step 2: Set timer for 1 min prep. Write 4–5 keywords only.
Step 3: Record yourself on phone. Speak 1–2 minutes.
Step 4: Replay. Count hesitations ("um", "uh"). Mark grammar errors. Mark repeated words.
Step 5: Re-record the SAME topic. Improve fluency + vocabulary.

Common mistakes that drop your band

  • 1-word answers in Part 1
  • Stopping early in Part 2 (lose marks)
  • Memorized speeches (examiner will deviate from script)
  • Speaking too fast when nervous → pronunciation suffers
  • Using basic words only ("good", "nice", "bad", "happy")
  • Saying "I don't know" in Part 3 → develop an opinion anyway
