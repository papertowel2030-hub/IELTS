Writing — Target Band 7.0

GT Writing = 2 tasks in 60 minutes. Task 1: letter (20 min, 150+ words). Task 2: essay (40 min, 250+ words). Task 2 is worth DOUBLE marks.

Task 1 — Letter (20 min, 150+ words)

  • 3 types: Formal (to a stranger / official), Semi-formal (to a known person formally), Informal (to a friend)
  • Tone MUST match the question prompt
  • Cover ALL 3 bullet points in the prompt

Task 1 template (use this structure)

Paragraph 1 — Reason for writing "Dear [Name / Sir / Madam], I am writing to [reason]." Paragraph 2 — Bullet point 1 + Bullet point 2 "To begin with, [explain]. In addition, [details]." Paragraph 3 — Bullet point 3 + action request "I would also like to mention that [point 3]. I would be grateful if you could [action]." Closing Formal → "Yours faithfully," / "Yours sincerely," Informal → "Best wishes," / "Take care," [Your name]

Task 2 — Essay (40 min, 250+ words)

  • 4 essay types: Opinion, Discussion, Problem/Solution, Advantages/Disadvantages
  • 4-paragraph structure: Intro → Body 1 → Body 2 → Conclusion

Task 2 template (use this structure)

Paragraph 1 — Intro (2 sentences) Sentence 1: Paraphrase the question. Sentence 2: State your position / preview your 2 points. Paragraph 2 — Body 1 (4-5 sentences) Topic sentence (main idea). Explanation. Example. Result / impact. Paragraph 3 — Body 2 (4-5 sentences) Topic sentence (second main idea). Explanation. Example. Result / impact. Paragraph 4 — Conclusion (2 sentences) Restate your position. Final thought / recommendation.

Band 7 checklist — your writing MUST have

  • Word count met (150+ for T1, 250+ for T2)
  • Paragraphs (NOT bullet points)
  • 5+ linking words: however, therefore, in addition, for example, on the other hand, consequently
  • 3+ complex sentences (with "because", "although", "which", "if")
  • Varied vocabulary (don't repeat the same word 5 times)
  • All parts of the question answered
  • Clear position (Task 2)

How to study — daily steps

Step 1: Get a prompt from IELTS Liz — 100 Task 2 prompts or Simon — Task 2 archive.
Step 2: Set timer to 40 min (T2) or 20 min (T1).
Step 3: Write by hand on paper (real test = paper). Time yourself.
Step 4: Count words. Check the template above. Did you follow it?
Step 5: Compare to a Band 7–8 model answer on ielts-simon.com.

Common mistakes that drop your band

  • Under word count → automatic penalty
  • Memorized phrases that don't fit the question
  • One huge paragraph (no structure)
  • Repeating the same words (boring vocabulary)
  • Not answering all parts of the question
  • Bullet points in Task 1 (must be sentences)
