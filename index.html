Step 11: Test day — body + mind protocol

Click to expand — logistics + cognitive prep

Night before

  • Sleep 7–8 hrs. No new material after 8 pm — review only.
  • Lay out: passport (same one used at registration), pen + HB pencil + eraser, clear label-free water bottle.
  • No alcohol, no heavy meals.

Morning

  • Protein + slow carbs (eggs, oats). Caffeine if normal for you, not extra.
  • Arrive 30 min early. Phone OFF (not silent).
  • Box breathing 4-4-4-4 if heart rate spikes. Yerkes-Dodson: moderate arousal helps, panic kills.

During test

  • Listening: write on question booklet during audio, transfer in the 10-min window. Bathroom before, not during.
  • Reading: pace = ~20 min Sec 1, ~20 min Sec 2, ~20 min Sec 3. Transfer as you go (no extra time).
  • Writing: 20 min T1, 40 min T2. Plan 3 min, write 33 min, check 4 min for T2.
  • Speaking: examiner is neutral. Smile, breathe, expand answers. End Part 2 naturally if you finish before 2 min — examiner stops you anyway.
