🚫 Anti-patterns — look productive, aren't ›
  • Watching strategy videos back-to-back instead of practising.
  • Re-reading notes (feels good, barely helps — test yourself instead).
  • Only doing Listening + Reading because they're easy to self-grade.
  • Writing essays you never submit for feedback.
  • Memorising "advanced" words with no collocations.
  • Skipping the error log because "I'll remember."
Reschedule rule: if a late mock still misses your target by more than 0.5 in any skill, move the test date. A few more weeks of focused prep is far cheaper than a retake.
